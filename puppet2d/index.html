Garment attachment

The front/back garment panels inherit the torso transform directly. The patterned cuffs inherit the wing pivots, so the owl can never move “under” a separate clothing shell.

Animation

Idle, wave, speech and celebration are authored as bounded keyframe clips. Blinks, eye direction and beak opening are separate facial channels.

2D-first

The design is intentionally a layered puppet: body, garment, wings, head, eyes, brows, beak, legs and tail. No 3D collision, drape or hidden cloth state remains.
